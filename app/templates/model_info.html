warning Scientific Limitations & Ethical Use Disclaimers
  • 1. Feature Correlation vs Causality: High feature gain for $\Delta\text{NIR}$ or $\Delta\text{NDVI}$ indicates strong model reliance on spectral divergence, NOT independent confirmation of physical construction or demolition.
  • 2. Non-Calibrated Probabilities: XGBoost output change probabilities represent relative decision confidence for the thresholded pixel cluster, not absolute real-world ground truth probabilities.
  • 3. Decoupled Semantic Reference: Semantic class labels originate from the reference multi-spectral land-cover pipeline. Classification precision depends on the underlying reference raster quality.
